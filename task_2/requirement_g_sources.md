## Citation Audit

The citations in Requirements D through F were reviewed against their corresponding reference lists and publisher or DOI metadata. The academic claims in those documents are supported by recent peer-reviewed journal literature, while model metrics, confusion matrices, and optimization results are original project outputs and are not attributed to external sources. Each in-text citation was checked for a matching reference, and each listed academic reference is cited in its associated requirement document.
